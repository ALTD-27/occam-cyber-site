Ask us something else →
What is an agentic SOC?

A security operations centre where AI agents do the repetitive first pass: pulling the logs, resolving who and what is involved, testing each possible explanation and proposing a response. A named person stays accountable and makes the call on anything beyond the limits you set.

How is an AI SOC different from a traditional managed SOC?

Many managed SOCs still have people working an alert queue. Here, agents do the first pass on the alerts in scope and write down why each explanation was ruled out, so people spend their time on decisions. You get the reasoning and the evidence, not just a ticket.

Which security tools does it work with?

Microsoft Defender, Entra and Sentinel, and CrowdStrike Falcon. We build on the agentic security tools we judge best for the job, tell you why we chose them, and scope against what you already run before suggesting anything new.

How does threat intelligence fit in?

Every alert is checked against what is known about the attackers, tools and infrastructure behind it, using the intelligence built into Microsoft Defender and CrowdStrike Falcon, plus public advisories such as those from the Australian Cyber Security Centre. How much of it you get depends on the licences you hold, and we tell you which feeds apply to you before you sign. We do not run our own intelligence feed. You can also engage us for threat intelligence on its own: exposure monitoring, attack surface monitoring, brand protection and supplier exposure.

Do the agents act on their own?

Only inside limits you set in writing. Anything beyond those limits waits for a named person to approve it, and every action and decision is recorded as evidence.

Is someone watching outside business hours?

Not by default. Hours of human cover are agreed with you up front, and we tell you plainly what agents do, and what waits for a person, outside those hours.

Who is the named analyst?

A named person on our team who is accountable for your service and for the decisions made on it. You are told who it is before work starts.

What access do you need to our tenant?

The minimum needed, agreed in writing before any agent acts. You keep ownership of the tenant.

Where is our data held?

Your Microsoft security data lives in your own tenant. Before we start we confirm it is set to an Australian region, and we tell you about any service that processes it elsewhere. For every other tool we use, including CrowdStrike Falcon, we tell you where your data is held before you sign, along with where the models that read your data run and where evidence packs are stored. We are an Australian company with an Australian team.

What happens when I get in touch?

You send a short request and we reply by email to arrange a conversation about what you run and what you need covered. If we are not the right fit, we will say so.

Do we need a particular Microsoft licence?

We do not start from a licence requirement. We scope against what you have today and tell you before anything costs extra. Some capabilities need specific plans, and some, such as Sentinel and Security Copilot, are billed on what you use, so we size and stage those with you.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you run and what you need covered, so we scope it first and put the fees in a Statement of Work you can sign. Anything that costs extra, including Microsoft usage-based billing, we show you before you commit.

Who is Occam Cyber for?

Mid-sized Australian organisations, roughly 50 to 500 people, that run Microsoft or CrowdStrike security tools and do not have the team to run them.
