WHAT THE DEMO COVERS

One alert, five steps, one decision.

  1. 01Observe

    Events arrive from endpoints, cloud, identity, SaaS and AI agents and become one operational picture.

  2. 02Understand

    The event is connected to the account, its owner, the data it can reach and the controls around it.

  3. 03Reason

    Every plausible explanation is tested against the evidence and ruled out, with the reason written down, until one remains.

  4. 04Act

    Anything beyond the limits you set waits for a named person. Agents propose, a person approves, agents carry it out.

  5. 05Assure

    Actions, approvals and outcomes are recorded as evidence you can show a board or an auditor.

What happens when I book a demo?

You send a short request, we reply by email to arrange a time, and we walk you through an example investigation and where a person decides.

Which security tools does it work with?

Microsoft Defender, Entra and Sentinel, and CrowdStrike Falcon. We build on the agentic security tools we judge best for the job, tell you why we chose them, and scope against what you already run before suggesting anything new.

Do the agents act on their own?

Only inside limits you set in writing. Anything beyond those limits waits for a named person to approve it, and every action and decision is recorded as evidence.

Where is our data held?

Your Microsoft security data lives in your own tenant. Before we start we confirm it is set to an Australian region, and we tell you about any service that processes it elsewhere. For every other tool we use, including CrowdStrike Falcon, we tell you where your data is held before you sign, along with where the models that read your data run and where evidence packs are stored. We are an Australian company with an Australian team.
