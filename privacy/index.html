Privacy notice

Plain English: what we collect through this website, why, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

This notice is from Occam Cyber, a trading name of 27 INVESTMENTS PTY LTD (ACN 675 457 020). It explains what we do with the details you send us through this website.

What we collect

When you use the form: your name, work email, company, company size, the security tools you say you run, and anything you write to us. With the form we also record the page you were on, how you reached us (the referrer and any campaign tags in the link) and the time. The site runs no analytics or advertising tags.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry and follow up on the conversation. We also look at which pages and campaigns lead to enquiries so we can improve the site.

Who sees it

Our team. Enquiries reach us as email, which is provided through Microsoft 365. We do not sell your details.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as we need them to reply and follow up. Enquiries that do not become clients are deleted within 24 months.

Cookies and storage

This site does not set cookies. It keeps the campaign tags from your link in your browser's session storage until you close the tab, so we can attach them to your enquiry if you send one.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you by emailing sales@occamcyber.com.au. If you are not happy with how we handle your details you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

We will update this page if what we do changes. Last updated: 18 October 2026.
